SAM eval (dev page, not part of the app)

Serve a directory that contains this repo's web/ and a link to the dataset (see ../analysis/eval.html), then open /web/js/sam/eval.html?data=/data/&n=30&step=10 (&backend=wasm|webgpu forces a backend). It takes every step-th labelled test image (up to n) and compares the automatic analyzer with SAM prompted by the ground-truth bounding box, by one point at the ground-truth centre, and by the box plus that point.
